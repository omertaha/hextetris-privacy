Privacy Policy for HexTetris

Developer: Tapnsense
Application: HexTetris
Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tapnsense ("we", "us", or "our") handles information collected through the HexTetris mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

1. Overview and Core Principle

Your privacy is important to us. HexTetris is designed as a standalone casual puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identifiable information to play the game.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

HexTetris does not directly collect, harvest, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • Your name, home address, or phone number
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Financial or banking information

3. Local Device Storage and Game Data

HexTetris stores game progression, settings (such as audio, vibration, and visual preferences), and high scores locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our own servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Platform Integrations

The App is distributed via the Google Play Store and is built using the Unity game engine. These third-party platforms may collect standard technical and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Google may collect anonymous device information, crash reports, installation metrics, and diagnostic data to facilitate app distribution, security verification, and performance analysis. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies: Unity may gather non-personally identifiable hardware and diagnostic information (such as operating system version, device model, and performance metrics) necessary to run the engine smoothly. For more details, consult Unity's Privacy Policy.

5. In-App Purchases and Payments

If HexTetris offers in-app purchases (such as removing ads or unlocking features), all payment transactions are handled directly through Google Play In-App Billing. Tapnsense does not receive or store any credit card, bank, or billing information.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HexTetris is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal information has inadvertently been collected from a child, please contact us immediately at tapnsense@gmail.com, and we will take prompt action to investigate and remove such data.

7. Data Security

Because we do not operate external user account databases or collect sensitive personal data, the risk of data compromise through our systems is minimized. Any local app data is subject to the security protections provided by your Android device operating system.

8. Your Rights and Data Inquiries

You have full control over your local data. You can reset or delete all game progress and locally stored preferences at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App through your device settings.

If you have any questions or wish to exercise any privacy rights, you can reach out to us at our contact email address below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
